Call of the Wild Anger is an open world fishing game. One of the off-book social challenges it to catch diamond of each and every species on each and every reserve.  
I used Google Gemini Carbon to walk through a a solution that I was unable to do myself after 4 years.   

The patch allows diamond for every water cell on all the maps, spawns only diamonds, and remove all hook size restrictions.  Bait restrictions are still enabled.
